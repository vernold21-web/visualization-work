TABLEAU CALCULATED FIELDS
Use all June-November 2026 rows for the graduate worksheet.
These names use the renamed Tableau fields Attendance, Capacity, Month, and Neighborhood.

Monthly Utilization
[Attendance] / [Capacity]

Average Monthly Utilization
AVG([Monthly Utilization])

Weighted Observed Utilization
SUM([Attendance])
/
SUM(IF NOT ISNULL([Attendance]) THEN [Capacity] END)

Month Coverage
STR(COUNT([Attendance])) + " / " + STR(COUNT([Month]))

Format both aggregate utilization fields as Percentage, 2 decimal places.
Keep missing attendance null. There is one row per neighborhood per month in this CSV.
